Privacy Policy

Usage Monitor for Claude · Last updated: June 12, 2026

Summary

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Usage Monitor for Claude has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app does with your data

  • Session credential: To display your usage limits, you provide a session cookie for your own claude.ai account. It is stored only in the macOS Keychain on your Mac and is sent only to claude.ai over HTTPS to read your usage data. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Local usage logs: If you grant access to your ~/.claude folder, the app reads Claude Code's local log files (read-only) to calculate today's token usage and estimated cost. This processing happens entirely on your Mac; nothing leaves your device.
  • Settings: Preferences (such as your organization ID) are stored locally in the app's sandboxed container.

Network connections

The app makes exactly one kind of network request: HTTPS requests to claude.ai to read your own usage information. No other connections are made.

Data deletion

Quit the app and delete it. To remove the stored credential, delete the "nz.co.worldway.usagemonitor" item in Keychain Access. No data exists anywhere else.
