Powerful Features

Everything you need to monitor and manage your Claude.ai usage effectively

⏱️

5-Hour Rolling Window

Track your usage within the 5-hour rolling window limit with real-time countdown and percentage display.

🛠️

7-Day All Models Tracking

Monitor your comprehensive 7-day usage across all Claude models with detailed statistics.

🔷

Sonnet Limit Monitoring

Keep track of your Sonnet model usage with dedicated 7-day limit tracking.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Receive timely alerts at 90% and 95% usage thresholds to help you manage your limits proactively.

🚀

Auto-Start on Login

Launch automatically when you start your Mac. Enable or disable with a single click in the menu.

🔒

Privacy First

All data stored locally on your Mac. No analytics, no tracking, no external servers. Your data stays yours.

⚡

Real-Time Updates

Automatic refresh every 1 minute keeps your usage data current. Manual refresh available anytime.

🆓

Free & Open Source

Completely free to use. Open source on GitHub. No subscriptions, no hidden costs, ever.

See It in Action

Clean, intuitive interface right in your macOS menu bar
